PrismGS

Coordinated Training and Compression for Pareto-Optimal 4D Gaussian Streaming

Demo Video

Abstract

Dynamic 4D Gaussian scene streaming requires balancing visual quality, storage cost, and transmission bandwidth under rapidly changing network conditions. PrismGS addresses this problem with a coordinated training-and-compression pipeline that models the rate-distortion behavior of different Gaussian attribute groups and selects Pareto-optimal compression configurations for streaming. Instead of relying on a single uniform compression level, PrismGS performs attribute-aware allocation across geometry- and appearance-related components, enabling more effective bitrate use under bandwidth constraints.

Overview

  1. Convert reconstructed 4D Gaussian checkpoints into feature images.
  2. Compress feature images into videos under different QP settings.
  3. Reconstruct anchor checkpoints from compressed feature videos.
  4. Measure attribute-wise sensitivity and fit a quality model.
  5. Generate Pareto-optimal compression configurations.
  6. Evaluate streaming quality and smoothness under real bandwidth traces.

Pareto Frontier

PrismGS explicitly models the rate-distortion tradeoff of Gaussian attribute groups and searches for Pareto-optimal operating points for bandwidth-adaptive streaming.
